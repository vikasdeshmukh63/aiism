Recruitment operations

Operational queues become available as approved recruitments are published and applications move through each stage.

Applications & eligibility screening

Post-specific application sections, duplicate-account prevention, document type/size/presence checks, age/qualification/experience validation and Application ID on submission.

Interview-based · no written exam

Shortlisting & PIS

Configure qualification, experience, publication and academic-performance weightage; calculate pre-interview scores, rank candidates and lock the shortlist with audited overrides.

Scoring configuration required

Interview management

Assign chairpersons and panel members, schedule interview slots, prevent panel conflicts and duplicate candidate slots, and prepare email/SMS interview notifications.

Panel setup required

Document verification

Review candidate documents and screening flags; record Verified, Rejected or Conditional decisions against the original evidence.

Verification queue

Merit & reservation-based selection

Combine pre-interview and interview scores, apply documented tie-breakers, validate category slots and prepare category-wise waitlists before approval and final lock.

Roster validation required

Results & offers

Prepare merit-list PDFs and candidate scorecards, issue offer letters and track offer acceptance and joining.

Final approval required
These operations are shown as the EOI workflow and are not represented as live services in this front-end prototype. Candidate data, integrations and notifications must be connected to the secured backend.
