How to Apply

Follow these three simple steps to apply for Faculty, Senior Resident, Junior Resident and Nursing posts at AIIMS Gorakhpur.

  1. 1Register on the PortalClick Login / Register and complete a one-time registration with your mobile number, e-mail ID and Aadhaar, verified by OTP.
  2. 2Apply for the PostLog in, choose an advertisement, fill in qualification and experience details, and upload documents or fetch them from DigiLocker.
  3. 3Track & Get UpdatesTrack your application and receive shortlisting, interview call letter and result updates by SMS and e-mail.

Important Instructions to Candidates

Detailed Instructions (PDF) »
  1. Applications will be accepted only through this online portal. Applications sent by post, e-mail or by hand will not be entertained unless specified in the advertisement.
  2. Candidates must register using a valid mobile number and e-mail ID. One candidate may hold only one registration.
  3. Candidates must fulfil all eligibility conditions as on the closing date mentioned in the advertisement.
  4. Category certificates (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS/PwBD) must be in the format prescribed by the Government of India.
  5. Mere submission of an application does not entitle a candidate to be called for interview. Candidature is provisional, subject to verification of original documents.
  6. Canvassing in any form will lead to disqualification.
Beware of fraud: AIIMS Gorakhpur does not appoint any agent for recruitment and never asks for money for selection. All official notices are published only on aiimsgorakhpur.edu.in and this portal.
