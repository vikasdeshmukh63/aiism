Before You Register
  1. Keep a valid mobile number and personal e-mail ID ready. OTPs will be sent to both.
  2. Keep your Aadhaar number ready. The UIDAI OTP goes to your Aadhaar-linked mobile.
  3. Enter your name and date of birth exactly as in your Matriculation / Birth Certificate.
  4. Only one registration is allowed per candidate. Duplicate registrations will be rejected.
  5. Registration does not mean you have applied. After registering, log in and apply for the advertisement of your choice.
